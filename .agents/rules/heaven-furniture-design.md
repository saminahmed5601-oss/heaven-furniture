# Heaven Furniture Mart Design Rules

Always treat Heaven Furniture Mart as a luxury bespoke furniture and interior styling studio.

Design language:
- quiet luxury
- editorial
- warm
- spacious
- premium
- craftsmanship-focused
- human

Never make the website resemble:
- ecommerce marketplaces
- SaaS dashboards
- generic templates
- cheap furniture stores

Preferred colors:
- charcoal-teal
- warm ivory
- muted brass
- deep brown
- natural wood

Typography:
- elegant serif headlines
- clean sans-serif body

Prioritize:
1. photography
2. typography
3. whitespace
4. hierarchy
5. craftsmanship
6. conversion

Avoid:
- excessive cards
- excessive rounded corners
- excessive gradients
- excessive shadows
- excessive animations
- excessive uppercase labels
- fake testimonials
- fake statistics
- invented awards
- invented products

Primary CTA:
Request a Quote

Secondary CTA:
WhatsApp Us

Always prioritize mobile quality and performance.
